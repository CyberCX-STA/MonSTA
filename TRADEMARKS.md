In this policy, these are collectively referred to as the "MonSTA Marks."

## 3. Uses that do not require permission

You may use the MonSTA name without obtaining prior permission when the use is truthful, descriptive, and does not suggest sponsorship or endorsement.

These statements must be accurate and must not be presented more prominently than the name of your own project or product.

## 4. Modified versions and forks

You may modify and redistribute the MonSTA source code in accordance with the software licence.

However, unless you have received prior written permission, a modified version, fork, or derivative distribution must:

1. use a name that is clearly different from MonSTA;
2. remove the MonSTA logo and distinctive project branding;
3. avoid representing itself as the official MonSTA software;
4. clearly state that it is independently maintained;
5. identify MonSTA only as the project from which the software was derived; and
6. preserve any notices required by the applicable software licence.

## 5. Unmodified redistribution

You may redistribute an unmodified, official release using the MonSTA name when:

* the software has not been modified;
* the source of the release is clearly identified;
* the original copyright, licence, and trademark notices are preserved;
* you do not suggest that you created or maintain MonSTA; and
* you do not suggest sponsorship, partnership, certification, or endorsement.

If you make any functional, behavioural, security-related, or user-interface modification, the resulting distribution must be treated as a modified version and renamed.

## 6. Domain names, organisations, repositories, and package names

Without prior written permission, you may not register or use the MonSTA Marks, or a confusingly similar name, as part of:

* a company or organisation name;
* a commercial product or service name;
* a domain name;
* a social-media account name;
* a GitHub organisation name;
* a repository presented as an independent product;
* a software package name;
* an application-store listing; or
* a conference, training, certification, or consultancy offering.

Repository names that merely identify a plugin, integration, or interoperability project may use descriptive wording where there is no reasonable likelihood of confusion.

Such repositories must prominently state that they are unofficial and independently maintained.

## 7. Logos and visual identity

The MonSTA logo and distinctive visual branding may not be used for a modified version, fork, product, service, website, merchandise, or promotional material without prior written permission.

Screenshots of the official software may be used for commentary, education, documentation, reviews, or reporting, provided the use does not imply endorsement.

Do not alter the MonSTA logo or combine it with another logo in a way that creates a new product identity.

## 8. Events, training, consulting, and hosted services

You may truthfully state that an event, course, consultancy, or hosted service relates to or uses MonSTA.

You may not use the MonSTA name as the primary name of a commercial service, certification, course, conference, or hosted offering without prior written permission.

Any permitted descriptive use must make clear that the offering is independently operated and is not endorsed by the MonSTA project.
